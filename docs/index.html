Hue Camera Viewer

Show a notebook, a book or an object to the whole class with your HUE camera. Rotate, zoom, freeze the image, and keep a copy when you need one.

Download for Mac macOS 13 and up
Download for Windows Windows 10 and up

Free and open source · All releases

Install

On a Mac

Open the downloaded file and drag Hue to Applications. On first launch, allow camera access.

On Windows

Run the installer, then open Hue from the Start menu. If SmartScreen shows a warning, choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

What it does

  • Your HUE camera is recognised and shown as soon as you plug it in.
  • Turn the image by a quarter turn; the app remembers it next time.
  • Zoom from 100 to 400 % with the slider, the scroll wheel or a pinch on the trackpad.
  • Freeze the image while you turn the page or move the camera.
  • Keep a picture in one click: a full-resolution PNG, on the Desktop.
  • Put the toolbar on any edge or hide it; everything works from the keyboard too.
